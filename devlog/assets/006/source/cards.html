Anatomy of a faction

Armada of the Last Line

  1. A name. Made up from the lucky number, in the style of its world.
  2. A signature color , plus a livery: does it go on the accents, the trim, or (like here) the whole body?
  3. A badge on every weapon. Seven shapes, endless emblems:
  4. A doctrine: favorite parts. This one loves bullpups, sleek bodies and loop grips, so the whole arsenal does too.
  5. Shared proportions and the same paint, finish and wear.
