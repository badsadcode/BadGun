Two new worlds

Same factory, very different neighborhoods.

Dieselpunk

Chrome speed lines, armor plate, rivets, radiators, engines.

Cyberpunk

Screens, neon, exposed cables, stickers, smart gear.

What's new on the shelf

15 new parts, 2 new sights.

DIESELPUNK

  • Searchlight sight, with a warm glowing bulb
  • Exhaust stacks on the muzzle, like a hot rod
  • Jerrycan magazine, X and all
  • Radiator handguard with cooling fins
  • Streamlined art-deco body and stock with chrome speed lines, or a riveted armored body with a valve wheel

CYBERPUNK

  • Smart-link sight with a screen facing you and a cable into the gun
  • Smart magazine: see-through window and an LED ammo counter
  • Corporate body with a built-in display, or a street-built one with stickers, neon tape and cables
  • Neon grip, blade stock, cabled handguard, smart muzzle
  • Plus a streamlined deco pistol and a smartgun pistol
