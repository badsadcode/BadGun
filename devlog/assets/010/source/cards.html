How snapping works

Every piece knows where it goes.

  • Every gun now has mount points: on top for sights, at the barrel tip, underneath, and on the side.
  • Every attachment has one matching point, its mount, right where it clamps on.
  • Line the two up and it fits. No rotating, no fiddling, on any gun the factory makes.
  • Lights and lasers even say where their beam starts, so a game knows where to shine.
